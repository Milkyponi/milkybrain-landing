Do you write in English or Indonesian?

Both. Indonesian scripts are adapted into English with voiceover for global audiences; Indonesian scripts stay in the register the audience already knows.

How fast is one script?

Nine videos a week at contract pace, sustained for a year at MMOPILOT. A single script with research usually lands inside three working days.

Who checks the facts?

Primary sources first: patch notes, developer posts, platform filings. An autonomous audit flags any claim it cannot source before a human reads the draft.

Do you take sponsorship work?

Yes, and it is labelled as sponsored. Eleven campaigns delivered since 2024, with the brief agreed before the hook is written.
